✦ Know Your Region

The Untamed
Eastern Crown

Vidarbha — the easternmost cultural region of Maharashtra — is a land where thick teak forests shelter India's most iconic tigers, where the perfume of orange blossoms drifts through winter mornings, and where Mahatma Gandhi chose to spin the wheel of freedom.

Comprising 11 districts across the Nagpur and Amravati divisions, Vidarbha is blessed with extraordinary biodiversity, a rich Gond tribal heritage, medieval temples, and the geographic heart of India — all within its borders.

✦ Places to Visit

TOP Destinations

From ancient temples to colonial era architecture, from dam viewpoints to forest trails — Vidarbha rewards every kind of traveller.

✦ Chandrapur
Tadoba–Andhari Tiger Reserve
Maharashtra's oldest and largest national park — highest tiger density in the state. Jeep safaris offer thrilling big-cat encounters.
✦ Wardha
Sevagram Ashram
Mahatma Gandhi's chosen home — a pilgrimage for every Indian, now a living museum of the freedom movement.
✦ Nagpur
🛕 Shri Mahalaxmi Jagdamba Temple, Koradi
One of the most revered temples in Vidarbha — dedicated to Goddess Mahalaxmi. Attracts thousands of devotees daily.
✦ Amravati
Melghat Tiger Reserve
Dense forests along the Gawilgarh Hills — home to tigers, sloth bears, leopards, and the Korku tribe.
✦ Nagpur
Ramtek Fort Temple
Ancient hill-top temple complex — the same Ramtek immortalised by the great Sanskrit poet Kalidasa.
✦ Nagpur
Pench
Pench National Park is another gem among the Vidarbha tourist places, known for its stunning landscapes and rich wildlife .
✦ Amravati
Chikhaldara Hill Station
Only hill station in Vidarbha — at 1118m, offering coffee plantations, waterfalls, and stunning valley views.

✦ Nature & Safari

WILD Vidarbha

Three tiger reserves, ancient forests, and a biodiversity that rivals the finest in Asia — Vidarbha is India's premier wildlife corridor.

🐯

Bengal Tigers

Vidarbha hosts 350+ tigers across Tadoba, Melghat, and Pench reserves — the highest density in the Deccan region. Morning safaris offer exceptional sighting rates.

🐆

Leopards & Sloth Bears

Dense forests shelter elusive leopards and the gentle sloth bear. Melghat is particularly known for its sloth bear population living among rocky outcrops.

🦚

Birdlife — 350+ Species

Indian rollers, Malabar pied hornbills, paradise flycatchers, and painted storks — Vidarbha is a paradise for birders, especially around Navegaon and Pench.

🌿

Teak & Bamboo Forests

Ancient teak forests interspersed with bamboo groves, mahua trees, and teakwood corridors that connect reserves and protect India's biodiversity heritage.

✦ Heritage & Traditions

LIVING Culture

Vidarbha's soul is woven from Gond tribal art, Varkari bhajans, medieval architecture, and the revolutionary spirit of Ambedkar and Gandhi.

01

Gond Tribal Art

The Gond people of Vidarbha create vivid, intricate paintings depicting nature, mythology, and daily life — now celebrated in galleries worldwide.

02

Ambedkar Movement

Nagpur's Deekshabhoomi is sacred to millions of Buddhists. The Ambedkarite movement transformed Vidarbha's social fabric profoundly.

03

Varkari Tradition

Pilgrimages to Pandharpur pass through Vidarbha — the devotional songs (abhangas) of the Varkari saints fill the roads every year.

04

Gondi & Marathi Folk

Gondi folk dances, Lavani performances, Tamasha theatre — Vidarbha's performing arts blend tribal and mainstream Maharashtrian traditions.

05

Weaving & Handicrafts

Chanderi-style cotton weaving, bamboo craft, warli-inspired motifs, and Paithani silk — Vidarbha's artisans carry forward centuries of skill.

06

Architecture & Temples

Markanda temples (8th century), Bhimashankar, the Ramtek complex, and colonial Nagpur's heritage buildings — layers of history in stone.

✦ Taste of the Region

VIDARBHA Cuisine

Fiery, earthy, aromatic — Vidarbha's food mirrors the land: bold, rustic, and deeply satisfying.

✦ Nagpur Special
Saoji Mutton / Chicken
The fire-breathing pride of Nagpur — a dark, deeply spiced curry made with a secret blend of 20+ spices. Not for the faint-hearted.
✦ Nagpur's Pride
Nagpur Oranges & Barfi
The GI-tagged Nagpur Mandarin — sweet, juicy, aromatic. The orange barfi made from fresh oranges is an irresistible local delicacy.
✦ Village Soul Food
Zunka Bhakar
Spiced gram flour (zunka) served with sorghum flatbread (bhakar) — the quintessential Vidarbha farmer's meal, robust and nourishing.
✦ Breakfast Classic
Tarri Poha
Nagpur's iconic breakfast — flattened rice (poha) topped with spiced black chickpea curry (tarri). A street-food institution, eaten with steaming chai.
✦ Traditional Vidarbha
Patodi Rassa
Gram flour dumplings simmered in a tangy, fiery peanut-based curry — a rustic dish that defines Vidarbha's bold palate.
✦ Tribal Tradition
Mahua Cuisine
The Mahua flower — sacred to tribal communities — is used in ceremonial drinks, laddoos, and traditional cooking passed down through generations.

✦ Traveller Services

WORLD-CLASS Facilities

Vidarbha has invested heavily in tourism infrastructure — ensuring safe, comfortable, and connected travel experiences.

✈️

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport

Nagpur's international airport connects to Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Bengaluru and 10+ cities. International flights to Dubai, Singapore and Middle East.

🚂

Railway Connectivity

Nagpur is a major railway junction on Mumbai-Howrah and Delhi-Chennai routes. Express trains connect all 11 districts. Jan Shatabdi to Mumbai in 8 hrs.

🛣️

National Highways

NH-44 (India's longest highway) passes through Nagpur. NH-6, NH-7 link Vidarbha to all major cities. MSRTC buses cover every district daily.

🏨

Forest Lodges & Resorts

MTDC-run forest rest houses and private eco-resorts at Tadoba, Melghat, and Pench offer immersive stays just minutes from the jungle.

⭐

5-Star Hotels in Nagpur

Radisson Blu, Le Meridien, Tuli Imperial, and The Pride — Nagpur offers international-standard hospitality for business and leisure travellers.

🏕️

Eco Camps & Homestays

Tribal homestays in Chikhaldara and Gadchiroli offer authentic cultural immersion. MTDC eco-camps provide budget-friendly nature retreats.

🏥

AIIMS Nagpur

One of India's premier AIIMS institutes — providing world-class medical care to the entire Vidarbha region with 24/7 emergency and specialist services.

🚑

Tourist Medical Helpline

Maharashtra Tourism helpline: 1800-111-363 (toll-free). Rapid response units at all major tourist sites. Government hospitals in every district HQ.

💊

Pharmacy Network

24-hour pharmacies in all city areas. Jan Aushadhi Kendras provide generic medicines at subsidised prices throughout Vidarbha.

🎫

Online Safari Booking

Tadoba and Melghat safari permits can be booked online via Maharashtra Forest Department portal. Zones open year-round except peak monsoon.

🌿

Eco-Tourism Guides

Certified naturalist guides available at all reserves. MTDC and private operators offer full safari packages including accommodation, meals and transport.

📡

Digital & Connectivity

4G network covers Nagpur, Chandrapur, Amravati. Forest zone Wi-Fi at major tiger lodges. UPI and digital payments accepted at all tourism points.

✦ Plan Your Trip

HOW TO Reach

Vidarbha is well-connected from all major cities — getting here is the easy part.

✈️

By Air

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur. 1.5 hrs from Mumbai, 1.5 hrs from Delhi. International flights to Dubai & Gulf.

🚂

By Train

Nagpur Junction — major junction on 4 national corridors. Howrah Mail, Rajdhani, Duronto connect to all metros. 8 hrs from Mumbai.

🚌

By Road

NH-44 (Srinagar–Kanyakumari) passes through Nagpur. MSRTC luxury buses from Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad. 12 hrs drive from Mumbai.

🗓️

Best Season

Oct–Feb: ideal for sightseeing (15–28°C). Oct–May: safari season. Feb–May: orange harvest. Monsoon (Jun–Sep): lush forests, fewer crowds.

📍

Zero Mile, Nagpur — the historic geodetic datum point marking the geographic centre of India. Installed by the British Survey of India in 1907, Zero Mile in Nagpur served as the reference point for all road distances across the country.
